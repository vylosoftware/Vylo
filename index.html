How VyloVault Protects Your Data

  • Your master password is never stored.
  • Key derivation with PBKDF2 (600,000 rounds) and a unique salt per account.
  • The entire vault is encrypted and authenticated using AES (Fernet).
  • After 5 failed attempts, the lockout time increases, even after a restart.
  • Automatic logout after inactivity.
  • The clipboard is cleared after a time you choose.

Install in Three Easy Steps

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I forget my master password?

There is no recovery option. Your master password is not stored, so nobody can decrypt your data. Keep your password somewhere safe.

Why does Windows show a warning?

The installer does not currently have a code-signing certificate, so Windows may not recognize the publisher. You can verify the SHA-256 checksum using PowerShell: Get-FileHash VyloVault_Setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256.

Where is my data stored?

In the .vylovault folder in your user directory. It is kept when you update or uninstall. You can export an encrypted backup in Settings.

Does VyloVault send anything to the internet?

No. VyloVault stores everything locally and does not require an internet connection.
